# Progression

**Status:** `STATED`

You take on **large bosses**. Bosses drop **material**.

Material feeds three stated verbs:

| Verb | Stated as |
| --- | --- |
| upgrade | improve something you have |
| refine | — |
| build | make new weaponry |

"Upgrade, refine, and build amazing weaponry" is the full stated scope. The
distinction between the three verbs has not been defined and must not be
assumed.

## This is the crafting pillar

"Craft gear" in [[Premise]] now has a source: boss material. That resolves where
gear comes from.

It does **not** resolve what scavenged flora is for. Flora gathering exists in
[[tap-grid]] with placeholder trait strings. Whether flora is a second material
track, a consumable track, or vestigial is [[Open Questions|open]].

## Large bosses vs. the viewport

**Status:** `STATED` — for mobile · `PROPOSED` — the rest

**On mobile the viewport enlarges for bosses.** Stated. See [[Platforms]].

The reason: a phone at thumb-legible tile size shows roughly 9×14 tiles
([[Visual Treatments]]). A "large boss" that reads as large has to occupy
multiple tiles — at 11 columns, a 5×5 occupant is half the screen. Desktop has
no such ceiling.

An ASCII boss that fills your screen is oppressive in exactly the right way, and
it is free — no art budget, no rig.

**Still proposed:** that bosses are multi-tile occupants, and that the mobile
enlargement is a fixed encounter framing rather than a continuous zoom. Boss
fights target far fewer distinct tiles than exploration does, so smaller tiles
cost less accuracy there than they would elsewhere. Untested.
