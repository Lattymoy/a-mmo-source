## Verified from project-raum (read 2026-08-22)

Recorded only so this project does not contradict established world facts:

- Raum is the first world. It carries three bands: **Non-Corrupted → Corrupted →
  Shaped**. The naming is the fiction — untouched world, then corruption, then
  something deliberately re-forming it.
- Palette, enemy roster, and prop language degrade per band.
- One faction is on record: the Lattice — the Cube's crystal overtaking the living.

Nothing further is imported. Anything beyond these lines must be read from
project-raum before use.

## What is this project's, not inherited

project-raum is a different game in the same world: a turn-based voxel roguelike
on a forward-only 5-lane strip, endless, distance as the progression metric.

This project is hub-and-instance with matchmade missions ([[World Structure]]).
The two share a world and share nothing structural.

**Known divergence to resolve:** project-raum has a hub of its own — a three-
building campsite you spawn at, single-player, marked COMPLETE. The hub in
[[World Structure]] is a shared social space with vendors, training, and quests.
Same world, two different hubs. Whether they are the same place, different
places, or different eras is not stated. See [[Open Questions]].
