# Premise

**Status:** `STATED`

A mobile game. Simple on the surface, complex under the hood.

The framing, in Mac's words:

- It starts with a single pixel.
- A grid — maybe ASCII, maybe just a simple black grid.
- You are inserted into a simple universe where ASCII monsters are taking over.
- You are a simple ASCII character.
- You traverse, craft gear, scavenge flora, and embark to fight the monsters head on
  with friends.

## Design intent

**Status:** `STATED`

Taking a step back from prior projects (2D and 3D geometry, complex mechanics).
Looking **simpler on the surface, deeper underneath**.

## What is not yet decided

Craft gear, scavenge flora and "with friends" are stated as pillars but have no
system behind them. See [[Open Questions]].
